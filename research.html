Research and Publications

Ongoing Research – Bilateral Movement Synergy Extraction Framework

Bilateral Movements of Upper limb

  • Develop a computational framework to extract and analyze motor synergies from bilateral (bimanual and bi-arm) movement datasets to understand coordinated control patterns between both limbs.
  • Identify the co-ordinates of upper limb and interlinking that to humanoid robot
  • Tech Stack:MediaPipe/OpenPose for pose extraction → Python (NumPy, SciPy, Pandas) for processing → scikit-learn (PCA/NMF) for synergy extraction → Matplotlib/Seaborn for visualization → PyTorch/TensorFlow for advanced modeling.

Ongoing Research – 5G/6G V2X Communications

Density-Aware Adaptive V2X Communication Based on Road Density

  • Developing a lightweight Context-Aware Adaptive Switching (CADS) algorithm for dynamic V2V/V2I mode selection based on vehicle density and link quality.
  • Incorporates hybrid metric optimization (density + RSSI + delay) and RL-based threshold learning for mobility-aware switching in 5G/6G networks.

Published — IJRTI Vol. 10 Issue 1 (2025)

Solution for Securing Sensitive Data: Breach Prevention & Mitigation

Co-authored with Dr. K. Sandhya Rani Kundra

  • Proposed a proxy-number and encryption-based framework to prevent Aadhaar-linked data breaches through SQL queries on encrypted data.

Automated Detection and Mitigation of DDoS Attacks in Cloud-Hosted Web Applications
